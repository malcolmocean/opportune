After onboarding: the things that used to be questions

These were steps in the flow earlier tonight. Each one is now either inferred, lazy, or a command.

Where I think the streamlining should go next

  1. Lead with the hook, not the setup. The invite already says "Jess wants to see more of you." The first thing the bot asks should build on that: "Want to see more of Jess too?" (one tap, sets wantMore both ways), then "Who else?" The calendar and location asks come after the person has said something they care about.
  2. Sources step is the long pole. Pasting an ICS URL on a phone is the worst moment in the flow. Two fixes: a one-tap "connect Google Calendar" OAuth button on the manage page (we chose to defer OAuth, but it's the right call for the second week, not the first), and making the Android app the default source for location so nobody pastes anything.
  3. Kill the porchlight question. Default to "ask me each time". The drop-in flow already graduates to auto-yes after three yeses. The question only adds a decision.
  4. App → Telegram should be a QR, not a paste. The app-first path exists server-side now (register, then "Link Telegram" deep link). The app UI for it is not built; today the app still expects a URL pasted from Telegram. That's backwards for anyone who installs the app first.
  5. Show one real nudge during onboarding. Right after the invite is accepted, the bot could say "Here's the kind of thing I'll send: You and Jess are both going to Sam's thing Saturday. Carpool?" People understand the product in one message instead of reading the pitch.
  6. Name confirm can disappear for Telegram users, since Telegram already gave us a name. Confirm silently; let them rename with "call me X".
